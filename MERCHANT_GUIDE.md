# Solo Convert Theme - Merchant Guide

Welcome to Solo Convert, the high-converting Shopify theme built specifically for selling **one hero product**.

---

## 🚀 Quick Start (5 Minutes)

### 1. Install the Theme

1. Upload the theme to your Shopify store
2. Go to **Online Store > Themes**
3. Click **Customize** on Solo Convert theme

### 2. Set Up Your Product Page

1. Navigate to **Products** in the Shopify admin
2. Click on your main product
3. Fill in all product details:
   - Product title
   - Description
   - Price
   - Compare-at price (for showing discounts)
   - High-quality product images
   - Variants (if applicable)

### 3. Customize Your Product Page

In the Theme Editor (Online Store > Themes > Customize):

1. Click on the **Products** dropdown
2. Select **Default product** template
3. You'll see all sections ready to customize

## 📋 Section-by-Section Setup Guide

### Hero Product Section (Above the Fold)

**Purpose:** First thing visitors see - make it count!

**Settings to Configure:**

1. **Main Benefit Headline**
   - Don't just repeat product title
   - Focus on the transformation or outcome
   - Example: "Sleep Better Tonight, Guaranteed" (not "Premium Pillow")

2. **Star Rating**
   - Enable if you have reviews
   - Or use static rating to build trust
   - Set rating (e.g., 4.5 stars) and review count

3. **Call to Action**
   - Keep it simple: "Add to Cart" or "Buy Now"
   - Enable "Buy Now" button for faster checkout
   - Consider "Get Yours Now" or "Start Your Journey"

4. **Payment Badges**
   - Upload icons for: Shop Pay, Apple Pay, PayPal, etc.
   - Text: "Secure payment options" or "Pay your way"

5. **Trust Signals**
   - Signal 1: Free shipping threshold
   - Signal 2: Money-back guarantee
   - Signal 3: Secure checkout or warranty

**Pro Tips:**
- Use a lifestyle image showing product in use
- Price should show savings if you have compare-at price
- Keep quantity selector visible for bulk buyers

---

### Benefits Section

**Purpose:** Answer "What's in it for me?"

**Settings to Configure:**

1. **Section Heading**
   - Question format works well: "Why Choose [Product]?"
   - Or benefit-focused: "What You'll Get"

2. **Add 3-6 Benefit Blocks**
   - Click "Add block" → "Benefit"
   - Use emojis or upload custom icons
   - Title: Short benefit (2-4 words)
   - Description: One sentence explaining benefit

**Example Benefits:**
- 🏆 "Premium Quality" - Made with medical-grade materials
- 🚀 "Fast Results" - See improvements in 7 days
- 💯 "Risk-Free" - 30-day money-back guarantee

**Pro Tips:**
- Focus on outcomes, not features
- Use customer language, not technical jargon
- 3-4 benefits is ideal; 6 maximum

---

### Image with Text Section (Storytelling)

**Purpose:** Problem → Solution narrative

**Settings to Configure:**

1. **Image Position**
   - Choose left or right
   - Alternate if using multiple sections

2. **Eyebrow Text** (optional)
   - Small text above heading
   - Example: "THE PROBLEM" or "YOUR STRUGGLE"

3. **Heading**
   - State the problem or solution
   - Example: "Tired of [Pain Point]?"

4. **Description**
   - Tell your story
   - Connect emotionally
   - 2-3 paragraphs max

5. **Add Key Points** (optional)
   - Use checkmarks or icons
   - Bullet-style highlights

**Pro Tips:**
- Use a lifestyle image, not just product photo
- First section: State the problem
- Second section: Present your solution
- Keep it scannable - short paragraphs

---

### Social Proof Section

**Purpose:** Build trust through customer testimonials

**Settings to Configure:**

1. **Section Heading**
   - "What Our Customers Say"
   - "Real Results from Real People"
   - "Join 10,000+ Happy Customers"

2. **Add Testimonial Blocks**
   - Click "Add block" → "Testimonial"
   - Customer quote (2-3 sentences)
   - Customer name (first name + initial)
   - Customer title: "Verified Buyer" or location

3. **Add Stats Block** (optional)
   - Number: "10,000+" or "4.8/5"
   - Label: "Happy Customers" or "Average Rating"

**Pro Tips:**
- Use real customer quotes (ask permission!)
- Include customer photos if possible
- Show 5-star ratings on each testimonial
- 2-3 testimonials + 1 stat works well

---

### Comparison Table Section

**Purpose:** Show why you're better than alternatives

**Settings to Configure:**

1. **Add Competitor Blocks**
   - Click "Add block" → "Competitor"
   - Name them generically: "Standard Brand", "Budget Option"
   - Maximum 2 competitors

2. **Add Feature Rows**
   - Click "Add block" → "Feature Row"
   - Feature name: "Premium Materials", "Money-Back Guarantee"
   - Your product: Yes/No
   - Competitor 1: Yes/No
   - Competitor 2: Yes/No

3. **Call to Action** (optional)
   - Button text: "Choose the Best Option"
   - Link to product (already on page, so optional)

**Pro Tips:**
- Be honest - don't make false claims
- Focus on your genuine advantages
- 4-6 comparison points is ideal
- Don't name competitors directly

---

### Guarantee Section

**Purpose:** Remove purchase anxiety with risk reversal

**Settings to Configure:**

1. **Badge/Icon**
   - Upload a guarantee seal/badge image
   - Or use emoji: 🛡️ 💯 ✅

2. **Headline**
   - "100% Money-Back Guarantee"
   - "Risk-Free 30-Day Trial"

3. **Promise**
   - One sentence: "Try it risk-free for 30 days"

4. **Details**
   - Explain the guarantee
   - Make it clear and simple
   - No fine print feeling

5. **Add Guarantee Features**
   - "No questions asked refund"
   - "Full 30 days to try"
   - "Fast refund processing"

**Pro Tips:**
- Make your guarantee bold and clear
- Longer guarantee = higher trust
- 30 days minimum recommended
- Actually honor your guarantee!

---

### FAQ Section

**Purpose:** Answer objections before they become problems

**Settings to Configure:**

1. **Add Question Blocks**
   - Click "Add block" → "Question"
   - Question: What customers actually ask
   - Answer: Clear, helpful response

**Must-Have Questions:**
1. What is your shipping policy?
2. What is your return policy?
3. How do I know if this is right for me?
4. Is this product safe/effective?
5. How long will it last?

**Pro Tips:**
- Answer honestly and completely
- Use customer language in questions
- 4-8 questions is ideal
- Update based on actual customer questions

---

### Sticky Add to Cart (Mobile)

**Purpose:** Keep CTA accessible on mobile

**Settings to Configure:**

1. **Button Text**
   - Match your main CTA
   - Usually "Add to Cart"

**How It Works:**
- Automatically appears when scrolling on mobile
- Shows product image, title, price
- One-tap to add to cart
- Desktop: Hidden (not needed)

**Pro Tips:**
- This is automatic - just enable it
- No customization needed beyond button text
- Huge conversion boost for mobile traffic

## 🎨 Global Theme Settings

### Typography

**Location:** Theme Settings > Typography

- **Primary Font:** Choose your brand font
- **Tip:** System fonts load faster (SF Pro, Roboto, etc.)
- Keep it readable on mobile

### Layout

**Location:** Theme Settings > Layout

- **Page Width:** Narrow (recommended) or Wide
- **Page Margin:** 20px default (comfortable spacing)
- **Tip:** Narrow width keeps focus on content

### Colors

**Location:** Theme Settings > Colors

- **Background:** Usually white (#FFFFFF)
- **Foreground:** Dark text (#333333)
- **Buttons:** Set in section settings
- **Tip:** High contrast = better readability

## 📱 Mobile Optimization Tips

1. **Test on Real Devices**
   - Use Shopify Mobile app to preview
   - Test on iPhone and Android
   - Check all tap targets

2. **Image Sizes**
   - Product images: 2000px wide minimum
   - Keep file sizes under 500kb each
   - Use JPG for photos, PNG for graphics

3. **Text Length**
   - Headlines: 5-10 words max
   - Benefits: 1-2 sentences
   - Testimonials: 2-3 sentences

## 🎯 Conversion Optimization Tips

### Headlines
- Focus on benefits, not features
- Use numbers when possible
- Create curiosity or promise transformation

### Images
- Show product in use
- Use lifestyle shots, not just white background
- Include before/after if applicable

### Copy
- Write like you're talking to a friend
- Use "you" and "your"
- Short paragraphs (2-3 lines)
- Bullet points for scannability

### Trust
- Real testimonials > fake ones
- Show faces if possible
- Be specific with numbers
- Authentic > polished

### Urgency (use honestly!)
- Limited stock (if true)
- Sale ending (if scheduled)
- Seasonal relevance
- Never fake urgency - damages trust

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Product form not working?
- Check that product has variants or is available
- Ensure form ID matches in sticky section

### Sections not showing?
- Make sure section is added to product template
- Check section isn't hidden in theme editor

### Images not loading?
- Upload images directly to product
- Or use Settings > Files to upload

### Mobile layout broken?
- Test in actual mobile device
- Check image sizes aren't too large
- Clear browser cache

## 💡 Advanced Tips

### Product Metafields
Add custom fields to products:
- Reviews rating (product.metafields.reviews.rating)
- Review count (product.metafields.reviews.rating_count)
- Custom benefits
- Extended warranty info

### Sections as Landing Pages
Use sections on standalone pages:
1. Create a new page
2. Use the product template sections
3. Custom landing page for ads

### Multiple Products (Not Recommended)
This theme is built for ONE product. If you need multiple:
- Consider separate stores
- Or use product variants cleverly
- Keep focus on hero product
